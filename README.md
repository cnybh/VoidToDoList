### 它是什么

VoidToDoList 是一个常驻在桌面上的待办清单。它不像普通软件那样占据一个窗口和任务栏位置，而是把自己"贴"在桌面上，安静地待在所有窗口的后面。你工作时它不会挡住任何东西，需要记录点什么的时候，它就在那里。

### 基本用法

打开程序后，屏幕右侧会出现一块半透明的黑色面板。

- 在底部的输入框里打字，按回车或者点右边的 **`+`**，任务就记下了。
- 做完一件事，点一下任务左边的白色小方框，它会打上勾、划上横线。
- 想改文字，**双击任务本身**就能编辑。
- 不想要了，点任务右边的 **`×`**，确认一下就删掉了。

没有分类、没有标签、没有优先级、没有截止日期——这些它都故意没有。它就是让你快速记下来、快速划掉。

### 两种"待机"方式，你挑一个

**固定模式**：面板一直待在桌面右侧，抬眼就能看见，适合把待办当成桌面便签用。

**抽屉模式（默认）**：面板平时收在屏幕右边缘外面，只留一个小小的把手。想看的时候点一下，它滑出来；再点一下，它滑回去。桌面干干净净，需要时才出现。

如果你喜欢把面板拉长一点、多显示几条，也可以在设置里打开"高度调节"，然后直接用鼠标拖住面板底边往上拉。

### 它会自己变透明

面板的透明度有五档可选。调到很淡的时候，它就像蒙在桌面上的一层影子，几乎不干扰你；文字会相应地变成黑色，保证还看得清。想要哪个程度，全凭你的桌面壁纸和使用习惯。

### 顺手生成一份工作总结

这是它最实用的一点。**在 `+` 按钮上点右键**，你可以让它把清单整理成：

- **今日总结 / 本周总结 / 本月总结**
- 或者你自己指定一段日期的总结

生成的内容里，已完成的任务会标注完成的日期，还没做完的任务会单独列出来。写完直接点"复制"，粘到邮件、聊天窗口或者日报模板里就行。

如果勾选了"**复制时附加 AI 提示词**"，复制出来的内容前面会自动加上一段整理好的提示词，直接粘给 AI 就能得到一份格式规整的工作汇报（包含概述、详细执行情况、后续安排、需要协调的事项四部分）。这段提示词的内容你可以自己改，点旁边的"编辑提示词"会用记事本打开，改完保存即可。

### 昨天的完成事项，第二天会自己消失

这条很省心。今天勾掉的任务今天还看得见，到了第二天，它们会自动从列表里挪到"历史记录"里，不再占着你的视线。但**记录并没有丢**——你生成周报、月报的时候，它们仍然会被统计进去。历史记录能留多久也可以自己定：2 个月、6 个月、1 年、2 年（默认）、5 年，或者永久保留。

### 说你的语言

内置 **18 种语言**，包括简体中文、繁体中文、英文、日文、韩文、法文、德文、俄文、西班牙文、阿拉伯文等。在设置里切换，界面立刻跟着变。阿拉伯语这类从右往左书写的语言，连界面方向都会整体翻过来，看着不别扭。

### 其他一些贴心的地方

- **开机自动启动**：设置里勾一下，以后开机它就自己在桌面等着了（不想要了取消勾选即可，不会在电脑里留下别的东西）。

- **换显示器也不跑丢**：插拔显示器或者改分辨率之后，面板会自动回到正确的屏幕位置。
- **数据是安全的**：你的任务保存在"我的文档"里的一个专属文件夹，不会因为你挪动程序而丢失。

### 怎么退出

在 `+` 按钮上点右键，选"退出"，确认一下即可。（直接点窗口的关闭是关不掉的，因为它本来就没有关闭按钮——这是故意的，免得误关。）

### What It Is

VoidToDoList is a to-do list that lives on your desktop. Instead of taking up a window and a spot in your taskbar, it tucks itself against the desktop and stays behind everything else. It never covers what you're working on — it's simply there when you need to write something down.

### The Basics

When the app opens, a translucent black panel appears on the right side of your screen.

- Type in the field at the bottom and press Enter or click **`+`** — the task is saved.
- When something's done, click the small white box to its left. It gets a check and a strikethrough.
- To change the wording, **double-click the task itself**.
- To remove it, click the **`×`** on the right and confirm.

There are no categories, tags, priorities, or due dates — deliberately. It's built for jotting things down fast and crossing them off fast.

### Two Ways to Keep It — Pick One

**Fixed mode**: the panel stays on the right side of the desktop, always visible. Good if you treat your to-do list like a desktop sticky note.

**Drawer mode (default)**: the panel rests just off the right edge of the screen, leaving only a small handle. Click it and the panel slides out; click again and it slides away. Your desktop stays clean until you need it.

If you'd like a taller panel showing more tasks at once, turn on "height adjustment" in Settings and drag the panel's bottom edge upward.

### It Fades Out of Your Way

The panel has five transparency levels. Turned way down, it's barely a shadow over your wallpaper; the text switches to black so it stays readable. How visible it should be is entirely up to you and your desktop.

### Get a Work Summary Without Writing One

This is the most useful part. **Right-click the `+` button** and have it turn your list into:

- **A daily / weekly / monthly summary**
- Or a summary for any dates you choose

Completed tasks appear with the date they were finished, and anything still open is listed separately. When it's ready, hit "Copy" and paste it into an email, a chat window, or a report template.

If you tick "**Add AI prompt when copying**", a ready-made prompt is attached ahead of the content — paste it into an AI and you get a properly structured work report (an overview, detailed execution, follow-up arrangements, and items needing coordination). You can rewrite that prompt yourself: click "Edit prompt", it opens in Notepad, and your changes are saved.

### Yesterday's Finished Tasks Disappear — But Aren't Lost

This one saves you a lot of housekeeping. Tasks you check off today are still visible today; tomorrow, they move out of your list into history, no longer crowding your view. **Nothing is actually deleted** — they still count when you generate a weekly or monthly summary. You decide how long history is kept: 2 months, 6 months, 1 year, 2 years (the default), 5 years, or forever.

### It Speaks Your Language

**18 languages** are built in, including Simplified and Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Russian, Spanish, and Arabic. Switch languages in Settings and the whole interface follows immediately. For right-to-left languages like Arabic, the interface flips direction entirely so it reads naturally.

### A Few Other Nice Touches

- **Start with Windows**: tick one box in Settings and it'll be waiting on your desktop every morning. Untick it to stop — nothing else is left behind on your computer.

- **It survives monitor changes**: plug in a new display or change your resolution, and the panel re-anchors itself to the right place.
- **Your data is safe**: tasks are stored in their own folder in Documents, so moving the app won't lose them.

### How to Quit

Right-click the `+` button and choose Exit, then confirm. (Clicking the window's close button won't do it — there isn't one, on purpose, so you can't close it by accident.)
