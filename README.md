一款纯粹的本地化效率工具，不联网、不注册、不付费。它隐身在桌面壁纸之下，与你的工作环境融为一体——任务清单直接“写”在壁纸层，开屏即见，专注无扰。极简交互，零学习成本，让待办回归纸笔般的直觉，却拥有数字化的清爽。你的数据只属于你自己，永远离线，永远安静。

A pure offline productivity tool — no internet, no sign‑up, no subscription. It lives seamlessly beneath your desktop wallpaper, blending into your workspace: your task list appears right on the wallpaper layer, visible the moment you unlock your screen. Minimal interaction, zero learning curve. It brings back the intuitive feel of pen and paper, with the crispness of digital. Your data stays yours — forever offline, forever quiet.
